# Billing FAQ — Factual Reference

Agent reads this when users ask about credits, pricing, or subscriptions. Only state facts listed here. For anything else, redirect without self-disclosure.

## Credits — General

- Credit consumption varies by model, video length, resolution, and generation method. **No fixed rate per operation.**
- All creation operations consume credits: generation, modification, regeneration, and conversation processing.
- There is no tool or API to query a user's credit balance or consumption history. **Never guess or estimate.**

## Credit Expiration

- **Subscription credits** (included with Pro): expire at the end of each billing cycle. No rollover.
- **Bonus credits** (sign-up rewards, promotions): have their own validity period as stated when granted.
- **Add-on credits** (purchased separately): have their own validity period as stated at purchase.
- Expired credits cannot be restored or compensated.

## Pro Plan

- Pro bonus credits (e.g. the 1,800 bonus) are a current promotional offer. Rules may change; refer users to the pricing page for latest info.
- Annual plans and more tiers are planned for the future.

## After Cancelling Subscription

- Plan stays active until end of current billing cycle.
- Subscription credits expire when the cycle ends. No refund.
- Bonus credits remain valid until their own expiry date.
- Add-on credits remain valid within their expiry period, but some features (e.g. watermark-free export) may require an active subscription.
- No further charges from the next cycle.

## Payment

- Processed via Stripe. Supports Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and other major cards.
- Available in most countries supported by Stripe.
- Issues → support@pexo.ai

## Subscription Management

- Cancel / update: Account → Manage subscription & billing → Stripe Customer Portal.
- Support: support@pexo.ai
